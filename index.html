Native Intelligence

Your Momo messages become your sales book. Nothing leaves your phone.

Download for Android
What it does

One feature, done well

Native Intelligence reads your MTN Mobile Money messages on your own phone and turns them into a simple business record book: sales, expenses and Momo fees, automatically, so you don't have to write them down by hand.

Automatic reading

Imports your last 90 days of Momo messages, then keeps reading new ones as they arrive.

Dashboard

Totals for today, this week and this month -- money in, money out, fees and net.

Manual entry

Add cash sales and expenses by hand, any time -- with or without Momo reading turned on.

You're in control

Turn off message reading or delete all your data at any time, right in the app.

Installing

About that warning

This app isn't on the Google Play Store -- not because it's unsafe, but because Play's review process for apps that read SMS messages takes longer than we have for this pilot. When you install the APK, Android will warn you that it's from outside Play and ask you to allow installs from this source. That's expected. Tap through it, then open the app.

The app itself will ask your permission before it reads any messages, and explains exactly what it reads and why before it does. If you'd rather not grant that permission, the app still works -- you can add your sales and expenses by hand instead.

Privacy notice

Your messages never leave your phone

What we read

Only messages from MTN Mobile Money ("M-Money"). Every other message on your phone -- personal texts, one-time codes, other banks -- is ignored and never stored.

Why

To log your sales, expenses and Momo fees automatically, so you don't have to write them down by hand.

Where it's kept

Only on this phone, in a database encrypted with a key that never leaves this phone's secure storage. Nothing is ever uploaded, backed up to the cloud, or sent to us or anyone else. The app has no internet access at all -- it is technically unable to send anything anywhere, even if it wanted to.

What we store

For each message we can read: the amount, fee, balance, who it's with, and the date. We never keep the message itself once it's been read successfully. If we can't read a message, we keep its text only until you resolve it by hand in "Unread messages" inside the app, then it's deleted.

How to stop or delete everything

Turn off message reading in the app's Settings at any time, or tap "Delete all my data" to permanently erase every entry and setting on your phone. Both are instant and irreversible.
